Apple Podcasts vs Spotify: Where to Focus Growth

Podcasters often ask which platform deserves the marketing budget. The honest answer depends on whether you care more about category charts, listening volume, or both.

Apple Podcasts: ranking prestige

Apple’s category charts are still a status signal for many sponsors, agents, and expert audiences. If your pitch deck needs “Top 20 in Business on Apple Podcasts,” Apple-focused ranking work belongs in the plan.

Spotify: listening behavior

Spotify rewards shows that fit listening habits inside its ecosystem. Growth here often shows up as stronger completion and discoverability among active Spotify users — valuable for download totals and habit-based audiences.

When to go dual-platform

Most established shows eventually need both: Apple for chart narrative, Spotify for listening scale. A 30-day dual-platform campaign is the standard structure when you do not want to choose only one.

  • New show, chart story matters → weight Apple first
  • Download volume for sponsors → include Spotify
  • Competitive category → longer timeline + dual signals
Related services Dual-Platform Growth and Apple Category Push are built for this split. Launch campaigns help new shows enter both ecosystems with early momentum.

Practical rule

Do not split effort evenly by default. Choose a primary platform for 15 days, then expand. Or run a designed dual campaign with weekly checks — not random posts on every network.
